# INC-0337 - Reviewers named at ruling time were busy when the artifact landed

**Cost:** Minor: caught before it shipped.

## Signature - how you recognise it

A review was reassigned because the reviewer named earlier was occupied.

## What happened

reviewers were named when a job was ruled and were busy when the artifact landed, so the review had to be reassigned twice

## Root cause

reviewers were named when a job was ruled and were busy when the artifact landed, so the review had to be reassigned twice

## Why it was hard to see

Naming a reviewer early looks like planning, and availability changes before the artifact exists.

## Fix

Name the reviewer when the artifact lands, from whoever is free and is not its author, and record the reassignment in the ruling

## Verification

Checked by the person who reported it. Not checked: whether the new rule prevents recurrence.

## Transferable rule

> Name the reviewer when the artifact lands, from whoever is free and is not its author; record the reassignment in the ruling
