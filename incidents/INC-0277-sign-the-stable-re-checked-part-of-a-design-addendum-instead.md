# INC-0277 - Sign the stable re-checked part of a design addendum instead of waiting

**Cost:** Minor: caught before it shipped.

## Signature - how you recognise it

Several authors were idle while one open part of a design addendum was pending.

## What happened

six parallel authors were blocked on a design addendum, so the lead signed the stable, re-checked part instead of waiting for the whole document

## Root cause

six parallel authors were blocked on a design addendum, so the lead signed the stable, re-checked part instead of waiting for the whole document

## Why it was hard to see

Signing a partial document feels like approving something unfinished.

## Fix

Sign every decision row that does not depend on the open items, and keep the held rows named

## Verification

Checked by the person who reported it. Not checked: whether the new rule prevents recurrence.

## Transferable rule

> Unblock parallel work by signing the stable re-checked part of a document and naming what stays held
