# INC-0431 - Interim content was signed as clearly marked placeholder copy instead of being invented as sourced

**Cost:** No invented copy entered the seed as if sourced.

## Signature - how you recognise it

Seed content needed text that no source provided.

## What happened

for seed content with no sourced text, the lead signed deterministic interim copy that carries a visible placeholder suffix, and interim labels recorded as a content-owner question rather than a sourced fact; the open item stays open for real copy and no synthesized marketing text is written

## Root cause

the inventory had gaps that only the content owner can fill

## Why it was hard to see

A plausible invented value would have looked finished.

## Fix

when a value has no source, sign a clearly marked placeholder, record the open question with its owner, and never present an invented value as sourced

## Verification

Checked by the lead. Not checked: whether the new rule prevents recurrence.

## Transferable rule

> Sign interim content as a visibly marked placeholder with its open question and owner; never present an invented value as sourced
